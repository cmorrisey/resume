## 1. Truth rules

Every line must survive "tell me more about that" in an interview. Recruiters for medical device, defense, and research roles ask follow-ups, and one inflated claim costs more than a missing keyword.

- **SolidWorks:** "SolidWorks through ME 228" or "experience through ME 228." Never "SolidWorks projects."
- **MeldMD tooling:** "built with the Google Places API." Never name a programming language.
- **Languages and tools Champ has not confirmed:** do not list MATLAB, Python, C/C++, Minitab, Arduino, LabVIEW, statistics. If a posting wants them, list them under "not claimed" and ask whether any are true.
- **NICU temperature probe adhesive (EMPOWER research):** Champ is the Marketing and Fundraising Lead and has not run tests, fabricated, or designed parts. Describe the team's work as the team's ("the team's prototypes"). He plans to join testing later.
- **AbleGamers controller (EMPOWER):** safe wording is "translate user needs into design requirements" and "develop/test/iterate 3D-printed, self-centering joystick prototypes; evaluate materials, manufacturability, and cost." "Against design requirements" is an inference; use it sparingly.
- **Quality Windows:** inspection, documented nonconformities, directed corrective rework before client sign-off, trained 3 employees. "Corrective rework," not "corrective action" (CAPA implies root-cause work he did not do).
- **Coastal Shine:** 40 jobs for 40 clients means no repeat clients; do not claim retention. Clients are homeowners and small businesses. He prepares cost estimates/quotes and handles sales. He maintains, troubleshoots, and repairs his pressure washing and soft washing equipment, sources replacement parts and supplies from vendors, and handles soft washing chemicals with required PPE (confirmed 2026-10-22). Do not claim calibration or formal preventive maintenance schedules beyond routine upkeep.
- **Regulatory-adjacent work:** 503A and 503B compounding pharmacies are the closest thing to regulatory experience. Do not claim submissions, registrations, post-market reporting, MRB, root cause, QMS, CTQ, UAT, process maps, or user guides.
- **Clearance:** DoD Secret, inactive, eligible for reactivation through Jan. 2028. Include only for defense or government roles; elsewhere keep just "U.S. Citizen" in the header.
- **Opinions and lessons in prose** (cover letters, essays): mark any sentence that states Champ's feelings or a lesson he "took" and ask him to confirm or rewrite it.
- **No em dashes anywhere.** Commas, periods, parentheses.
